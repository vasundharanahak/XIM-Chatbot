The University

Xavier University was established on July 6, 2013 as per the provisions of the Xavier University, Odisha Act, 2013. On 11th May 2021, the Xavier University, Odisha (Amendment) Act 2021 was passed in the Odisha Legislative Assembly, renaming Xavier University as XIM University. XIM University consists of thirteen schools: Xavier Institute of Management, School of Human Resource Management, School of Rural Management, School of Sustainability, School of Human Settlement, School of Commerce, School of Communications, School of Economics, School of Computer Science and Engineering, Xavier Law School, School of Governance and Public Affairs, and School of Liberal Arts

.

How can the application form for XIM University programs be filled up?

XIM University application form can be filled up through online mode only hosted on our website www.xim.edu.in . You will, therefore, need access to an internet enabled computer to apply to our program(s).

I am continuing Class - 12. Can I apply to XIM University now for the UG program(s)?

Yes, you may apply to XIM University’s UG program even if you are continuing your Class – 12; subject to completing the final part of the board examinations and expecting your final results during the year 2026 with a minimum percentage of marks (refer Table-1).

Is there a maximum age limit for applicants of UG program(s)?

No, there is no upper age limit for the prospective applicants.

I want to apply for more than one UG program at XIM University. What is the process?

You can surely apply for more than one program at XIM University, but you will have to choose the individual programs in the online application form. The total application fee will be calculated by the system. You need to use a single form for applying to multiple programs.

Do I have to get ready with any documents before filling the online application form?

Yes, before filling up the online application form get ready with a valid email ID, cell no., academic credentials, and the digital copy (in prescribed size/pixel format) of your pass port size photograph and signature.

Can I edit the application form after submitting?

No, once you submit the filled application form, editing of data is not possible.

After applying online successfully, do I need to send any hardcopy (e.g. my academic transcripts/mark sheets, printed copy of the application form, other documents and photographs) to XIM University?

No, you need not send any hardcopy of the documents/credentials to XIM University until and unless asked to do so.

However, in case you have filled the application before the announcement of your Class-12 result, you must report us your academic transcripts/mark sheets, via email to [email protected] immediately after the announcement of your Class-12 result. You are required to produce the original credentials at the time registration process.

How much is the application fee?

The application fee is INR 1500.00 for the first choice/option and INR 300.00 each for every subsequent choice/option. The application fee is non-refundable in nature.

INTERVIEWS / RESULT

What are the criteria for selection into the Undergraduate Programme?

Shortlisted candidates will be invited for written and or / PI through registered e-mail.

Do you conduct Written Test/Personal Interview as a part of the admission process?

For Xavier Law School and School of Computer Science and Engineering, written Test / Personal Interview is conducted as a part of the admission process.

Where will I get information on the final selection?

Selected candidates will receive offer for Provisional Admission through registered Email.

ADMISSION

How do we indicate the acceptance of the Admission Offer?

The selected candidate must book the seat by paying an offer Acceptance Fee as mentioned in the offer letter. The details/schedules of the fee payment can be referred from Provisional Admission Offer.

Can I defer my admission?

No, there is no provision to defer the admission. The conditions for accepting the admission offer will be mentioned in the offer letter.

What is the refund amount in case someone wants to forego the provisional admission?

The amount will be refunded after deducting the administrative expenses within 4 weeks, the details will be given in the provisional admission offer letter.

How can my admission be cancelled?

This admission offer and/or admission will stand cancelled if you –

* Have not complied with provisional acceptance date, or

* Do not register for the first academic semester on the given date, or

* Do not produce the required certificates at the time of registration, or

* Do not complete the requirements of your intermediate examination before the date of registration in this University.

HOSTEL

Are hostels compulsory for students taking admission for Under-Graduate programs in XIM University?

For UG Programs, hostels are not compulsory.

Can a localite avail the hostel? If yes, the amount he/she needs to deposit?

Outside Odisha students are preferred first followed by outside Bhubaneswar students and then local students. So yes local students can avail hostel and the hostel fees can be check from the university website.

What are the restrictions for undergraduate students to report back to campus?

 At XIM University, we take the safety of all our students seriously. Students are not allowed to leave campus without prior warden/prefect permission, which is then reviewed and approved by the Hostel In charges.

What is the approximate food expenses?

The approx. expenses will be around Rs. 4000-5000 per month. The student will have to incur other food and/or miscellaneous expenses and the same will not be collected by the University. The students have to pay to the mess contractor directly.

Please let me know about the residential facilities at XIM University?

XIM University has the world class residential Facility.

  • Boys & Girls have separate residential Building (HOSTEL).
  • Each Hostel have their own mess and is managed by a student’s committee and serves both vegetarian and non-vegetarian meals.
  • A 24-hour internet connectivity through the latest Wi-Fi technology, laser printing and photocopying machines are available.
  • Multi-purpose hall, co-operative store, book club are available.
  • Washing machines, water coolers, geysers, telephones, televisions, undisturbed power supply, different indoor games space are present.
  • A campus doctor, nurses, 24×7 ambulance for medical emergency are also available.
  • CCTV surveillance, security people for safety, bank branch and ATM machines etc.

Are there any food court/retail shops on campus? If not, how far do students have to travel to purchase household goods and other essentials?

 

A food court/retail shops are available on campus for students to purchase both fresh and packaged food, and other general provisions.

Are there any tuck shops on campus? If not, how far do students have to travel to purchase household goods and other essentials?

A food court/retail shops are available on campus for students to purchase both fresh and packaged food, and other general provisions

CLASSES

What are the timings of classes in the University?

Classes are usually conducted between 9:00 am and 5:00 pm. However, please note that classes might be conducted as required by Professors / visiting professors.

What about the faculty strength in XIM University?

There are more than 140 full-time faculty members in XIM University, apart from a big pool of eminent part-time and visiting faculty from academia and industry.

Are XIM University Professors accessible?

XIM University Professors are very accessible. Every professor holds weekly office hours. In cases where the student is unable to meet during the office hours, he/she may call or email the professor to set up an appointment.

Do students fail academically at XIM University?

Students accepted to XIM University prove to excel in their school studies. The great majority complete their undergraduate program within prescribed years. At any time, less than one percent of XIM University undergraduates are on academic probation as a result of poor performance and even fewer are placed on mandatory leave of absence, or dropped from the program for academic failure. XIM University is characterized by high level academic rigour. The student may fail in certain subject based on their academic performance, the details of which will be provided to the students in the Manual of Policies (MOP).

If a student is not so good at a particular subject, how and how often can he approach a professor to work on his or skills in the area?

Apart from the academic sessions, students are free to approach professors for one-on-one sessions on areas of difficulties for all subjects. If the professor should be unavailable during a particular period, the Dean’s Office will help students with all their queries as well as work with them for a longer period to overcome any challenges if they should need assistance.

Will the University’s academic timetable permit students to undergo training in a sport or extracurricular activity outside the campus?

The course is rigorous and undergoing training outside campus will be difficult for students. However, students can opt for training facilities available on campus.

If a student completes his/her UG course from XIM University will he/she get any preference for MBA at Xavier Institute of Management / XIM University?

The rank holders i.e.; 1st, 2nd and 3rd position holders of any discipline are shortlisted under rank holder category for MBA-RM, MBA-SM, MBA-UMG, MBA-PPM. Admission Interview for MBA – RM, SM, UMG, PPM programs without the Admission Test scores (CAT, XAT, GMAT, NMAT, XGMT etc.) will be conducted. Other candidates have to appear for CAT, XAT, GMAT, NMAT & XGMT (conducted by XIM University) for MBA-BM, MBA-HRM, MBA-RM, MBA-SM, MBA-UMG, MBA-PPM. However, the selection/ shortlisting to MBA programs is typically done based on the test scores, academic credentials, and personal interview. There are other PG Programs i.e.; M.Tech – CSE, M.Sc- Economics, MA-Mass Communication, M.Sc Psychology, M.A English and LLM which can be applied after UG Programs

MISCELLANEOUS

How far is the New Campus of XIM University from important destinations?

The distance of the New Campus of XIM University from Bhubaneswar Railway Station and Biju Pattnaik International Airport, Bhubaneswar, is 17 kilometers and 15 kilometers respectively.

What are the facilities available at XIM University?

The Old Campus and New campus of XIM University is situated in 55 acres of well-maintained compact campus with all the facilities befitting a world-class B-School. The campus houses air-conditioned classrooms with audio visual facilities and LCD projectors, spacious libraries with over 81,000 books and subscribe to over 20,000 journals (including electronic journals), separate hostels for boys & girls in the premise with LAN nodes in every rooms, Mess facility with vegetarian & non-vegetarian meals, 24-hour internet connectivity through the latest Wi-Fi technology , 3600 MBPS leased line internet support, round-the-clock printing & copying facility, campus doctor, nurses and ambulance for 24×7 aid, security guards and CCTV surveillance. The recreational facilities include cricket & soccer fields, floodlit basketball & lawn tennis courts, indoor badminton hall, gymnasium, jogging tracks etc.

Do students have the options to pursue extra-curricular and co-curricular activities?

XIM University has an active Student Life department which is committed to engaging students outside of the classroom. We currently have several student-run clubs and societies on campus, in addition to music, arts, dance, and sports opportunities.

Is there any transport facility provided to the students. If yes, then the approximate amount?

Yes, students can avail the transport facility on payment basis. The actual amount will be intimated in due course. These buses will ply from Bhubaneswar City to Campus and back.

Is a Laptop Computer mandatory for a UG student of XIM University?

It is mandatory to possess a laptop, while you are a student of School of Computer Science and Engineering. But it is not mandatory to possess a laptop for other programs/schools, while you are a student of the UG program of XIM University. But you may own a laptop for enhancing your competitional abilities and facilitate other academic activities.

Does XIM University has a safe campus environment?

The key to a successful learning environment is structure. XIM University houses solar-powered and air-conditioned classrooms, labs, multi-media halls, with excellent ventilation and ample natural lighting. The classroom complex features public address system, ergonomically designed age-appropriate furniture, CCTV surveillance system, wide corridors and stairwells, fire prevention system and deployment of security personnel. XIM UNIVERSITY is committed to the well-being and safety of our students, faculty, and staff. The University has taken enough measures to handle the Covid-19 Pandemic situation.

Does XIM University offer a distance education program?

No, XIM University does not offer any distance education program. All programs are full-time at XIM University.

Does XIM University have scholarship provisions for UG Programs?

Yes, XIM University does have Scholarship provisions for UG Programs. Refer below for the details:

  • Scholarship for the SC, ST, ECO BW/MINORITY – 3 each/year/program with 30% of the course/tuition fee as scholarship for 1st year+.
  • Meritorious scholarship – topper of each university program at the end of the year will get scholarship worth 30% of the course/tuition fee as scholarship+.
  • Students with 90% score in +2 examination will be provided scholarship worth 30% of the course/tuition fee only. This offer will be continued if the student secures a minimum CGPA of 8.5 in each of the subsequent years.+
Why UG at XIM University?

If you already know your direction in life, XIM University will put you, miles ahead on your career path. If your interests are broad and varied, XIM University will help you fully explore a range of options while developing fundamental business skills. The XIM University education will help you transform personal interests and academic passions into career opportunities. You will be able to assume a responsible position sooner than other college graduates who lack a rigorous grounding in the fundamentals of business.
