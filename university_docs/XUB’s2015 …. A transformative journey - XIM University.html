XUB’s2015 …. A transformative journey

XUB’s2015 …. A transformative journey

  • July 5, 2015
  • by XIM University

XUB’s2015 …. A transformative journey. As we bow out 2015 in thanksgiving and stretch out to embrace 2016, a quick look at how far we’ve come and ….miles to go!

During 2015, we welcomed on-campus two new School’s – Xavier School of Sustainability, inaugurated on July 5, 2015 & Xavier School of Communications inaugurated on July 2, 2015, offering Doctoral, Masters and Undergraduate courses. Both Schools offer Masters Programs in their respective areas.
