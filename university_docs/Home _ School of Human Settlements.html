School of Human Settlements

The School of Human Settlements is the University’s leading initiative to develop, transfer and disseminate knowledge about building sustainable, smart and liveable cities.. Research focuses on policy and institutional development, governance, planning and management of human settlements in rapidly urbanising geographies. Professional education and capability development include graduate degree, diploma and certificate programs to create expertise for urban development.
