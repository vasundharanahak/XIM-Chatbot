XIM UNIVERSITY
A Brief History


The Government of Odisha and the Odisha Jesuit Society in 1987 entered a "Social Contract" which led to the establishment of the Xavier lnstin1te of Management, Bhubaneswar. Xavier Institute of Management, Bhubaneswar is acknowledged internationally as a world class business school which provides quality management Programs anddevelops fun1ristic managers and leaders with strong ethicsandvalues.
Owing to its unique quality, brand and academic rigour, in its endeavour to take up the tasks whichare bold but necessary and which hitherto nobody has taken up XIMB, rose to meet the need for quality education in the state and c-0untry and founded the Xavier University. The Xavier University came into being with the Government of Odisha passing the Xavier University, Odisha, Act, 2013 and the New Campus was inaugurated on July 7, 2014. Subsequently, the stakeholders felt it prndent that the University would be better served by taking thename of its founding unit and accordingly the Xavier University renamed as XIM University by enactment of Xavier University, Odisha (Amendment) Act, 2021, with effect from May 11,2021.
XI M University is created to encompass variety and depth in the functional areas of management, technology and leadership, in the knowledge space of higher education.The identity of the University is distinguished by its philosophy of'lnspiring Futures' bynot just walking the well- trodden path but striking out and exploring new paths. The University also has a clear intent to be the first to respond to the needs of society and enable the building of sustainable communities that inspire the future of the state and the country, serving as an active agent of change. As a result, it is thefirst university to meet the need and demand of sustainability by establishing a School ofSustainability.
The University offers unique courses at the Undergraduate, Masters and at the Ph.D. level and has established schools like the School of Human Settlements, School of Governance and Public Affairs, School ofsustainability and others. 19. RAGGING
Ragging constitutes one or more of any of the following acts:
19.1. Any conduct by any student or students whether by words spoken or written or by an act. which has the effect of teasing, treating or handling with rudeness a fresher or anyotherstudent.
19.2. Indulging in rowdy or undisciplined activities by any student or students which causes or is likely to cause annoyance, hardship, physical or psychological harm or to raisefear or apprehension thereofin any fresher or anyotherstudent.
19.3. Asking any student to do any act which such student will not in the ordinary course do and which has the effect of causing or generating a sense of shame, or torment or embarrassment so as to adversely affect the physique or psyche of such fresher or anyotherstudent.
I9.4. Any act by a senior student that prevents, disrupts or disturbs the regular academic activity of any otherstudentor a fresher.
19.5. Exploiting the services of a fresher or any other student for completing the academic tasksassigned to an individualor a group of students.

19.6. Any act of fmancial extortion or forceful expenditure burden put on a fresher or any otherstudent by students.

19.8. Any act or abuse by spoken words, emails, post, public insults which would also include deriving perverted pleasure, vicarious or sadistic thrill from actively or passively participating in the discomfiture to fresher or anyotherstudent.
19.9. Any act that affects the mental health and self-confidence of a fresher or any othersnident with or withoutan intent to derive a sadistic pleasure or showing off power, authority, or superiority by a studentover any fresher.

INSTRUCTIONS FOR OFFLINE EXAMINATIONS FOR STUDENTS
I.  Astudent should report at theexamination hall at least 15 minutes before thescheduled time of the exam. A student will not be allowed into examination hall after the commencement of theexamination. If a student is late inarriving at theexam venue the Chief Invigilator/Invigilator is at the sole discretion to allow or disallow such late comers. It is the primary responsibility of thestudent to beavailable at the exam venue well ahead in time
2. Allstudents must bring their Identity Card to the Exam venue, and it should bekepton their desk during theentire examination. Failure to do so may render the student to be expelled from theexam venueandexamwill becancelled.
3. Each student is expected to bring their own writing equipment/aid including ruler, calculator, etc., into the examination hall, as pennitted by the instructor. Exchanging/borrowingof writing equipment/aid from otherexaminees is not pennitted at any time during theexamination.
4. Carrying of mobile phones or any electronics/bluetooth device including smart watch,health watch etc. into the examination hall is strictly prohibited. Students are also not allowed to keep mobile phones / any electronics gadget with them even in switched off or silent mode during examination. If a student has any kind of electronic gadgets/ mobile phone with him/her then s/he must deposit it with the invigilators before theexamination begins. Failure to adhere to this rule will immediately cause the exam to stand cancelled and the student will be asked to leave the examination hallby the Invigilators after filling the incident report.
5. Any electronic gadget/mobile phones confiscated during theexam shall remain with the COE Office and returned to the student only after disciplinary process is over. The University shall not be responsible for any loss of any electronic items confiscated during the examination. Students are, therefore, advised to refrain from carrying,possessingor wearing any electronic devices to theexamination hall.
6. A student attending any examination may be frisked before, during or after the examination if deemed fit by the Invigilators or any other appropriate staff of the University.
7. All tests are "closed book" unless explicitly specified otherwise by the faculty concerned with priorintimation to the Dean's Office andthe Controller of Examinations Office. No paper, books, or notes, etc. are allowed into the examination hall, except when thetest isdeclared to be"open book."
8. Any notes/incriminating documents found in possession or recovered from students or any exchange of remarks, notes or purposeful gestures, or glances at anotherstudent's paper in the examination hall will be considered as an attempt to cheat and will be treated as a malpractice. The invigilator will report this to the Controller of Examinations in the prescribed Incident form where both thestudent andthe invigilator will berequired to sign.This will immediately cause theexam to stand cancelled andthe

student will be asked to leave the examination hall by the Invigilators after filling the incident report.
9. Except for essential communication with the invigilator, a student is not pennitted to communicate in any fom1 with anyone during the examination. Passing or receiving information in any fonn or peeping into the answer books of others during the examination constitutes misconduct and is strictly prohibited. A student, who assists or abets giving or passing of infonnation, in any fonn whatsoever, will be considered as committing malpractice and could be expelled from the examination hall by the Invigilators.
10. No student will be allowed to go to washroom during examination in first 45 minutes and last 30 minutes of the examinations. However, in case of any exigencies only, a student may be allowed to go to washroom by the invigilator, but this will be after recording the out-time and in-time by the invigilator.
11. Any student spending more than 5 minutes in the washroom may be disallowed by the invigilator and the examination may stand cancelled and the student asked to leave the exam venue.
12. Only one person at a time will be allowed to go out of the examination hall to the washrooms. The invigilator will monitor the students and may record the time taken by the student in the washroom. Invigilators' observation will be considered as full and finaland no negotiation will be entertained.
13. Students must fill the particulars such as name, roll no., etc. on the cover page of the answer booklet as soon as it is distributed, before proceeding to ans,ver any question.
14. Students must stop writing as soon as the invigilator signals the end of the time allotted for the examination failing which the answer sheet may be rejected and cancelled.
15. All examinees must keep their Answer Sheets on their writing desk as per the seating order assigned to them.
16. Any violation of the nonns of examination will be considered a serious offence and an act of misconduct and will be reported in the prescribed fonnat by the invigilator to the Controller of Examinations for necessary action.
17. In caseof any violation of thecode of conduct, appropriate action will be taken as perthe extant provisions of the Student Manual of Policies or as deemed fit by the appropriate authority of this University.
18. All students of this University are sincerely advised to take examination matters and the above mies very seriously to avoid any situation of malpractice and the consequent implication.
