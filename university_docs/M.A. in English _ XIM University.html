MASTER'S PROGRAM IN ENGLISH
(MA-English) OFFERED
BY SCHOOL OF LIBERAL ARTS

Eligibility

Bachelor's degree of minimum three-year duration (or equivalent) in any discipline, from a recognized University, with at least 50% marks in aggregate. Final year degree students, who expect to complete their final examinations and other requirements for obtaining the degree by June 30, 2026, can also apply.

Application

Applicants are required to fill up the online application form at: www.xim.edu.in . Upon successfully filling the online application form and paying the relevant application fee, the applicant will be intimated by email about the acceptance of the form.

Selection Process

Eligible candidates will be invited for a personal interview to assess their academic readiness, motivation, & suitability for the program. The interview may be conducted either in person or virtually.

Admission

The program will begin by the first week of July 2026 for the first year students. At this time the students will be given a Manual of Policies and Regulations which will be binding on them.

Application Deadline

The last date to fill up the application form is March 31, 2026.
